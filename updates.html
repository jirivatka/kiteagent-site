Email updates

One address, so we can tell you when Kite ships and when something worth knowing changes. That is the whole purpose.

What you are agreeing to. Occasional email from RedPixl s.r.o. about Kite Agent — realistically a handful a year, and none at all until there is something to say.

  • Only your address is stored, with the date and the sentence you agreed to. No name, no IP address, no tracking pixels, no idea whether you opened anything.
  • Nobody else gets it. Not sold, not shared, not used to advertise anything but Kite.
  • Kept until you leave, and gone the moment you do.

Leaving

Enter the same address and it is deleted immediately — no confirmation step, no “are you sure”, no link to click in an email.

Prefer no email at all? The support forum announces the same things, and costs us nothing about you.
