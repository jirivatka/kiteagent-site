Your Hermes agent, on your phone.

Kite is an iOS client for Hermes, the open-source agent from Nous Research. It connects to the Hermes agent running on your own Mac — with its memory, its tools and your files — and puts it in your pocket. Scan one code and you’re connected.

Connecting is the whole point

Every other way of doing this asks you to set up a tunnel, a certificate, or a hostname first. Kite asks you to scan a code. It finds your computer on your network, and away from home it uses your own private network — so your agent is never exposed to the internet.

What it does

  • See what it’s doing. Searching, reading, running commands — the steps appear as they happen, not just the answer.
  • Know when it’s done. A notification when a long task finishes, and a live view on your Lock Screen while it works.
  • Send it anything. Share a link, a photo or a document from any app straight to your agent. PDFs are read on your phone, including scans.
  • Pick up where you left off. Conversations are shared with your desktop, your terminal and anything else that talks to your agent.

Your data stays yours

There is no Kite server. Your conversations go between your phone and your own computer, and nothing passes through us — we operate no backend for this app. No analytics, no tracking, no account.

Notifications carry no message content, only the fact that a reply arrived. See the privacy policy, which is short because there is little to describe.

No agent yet?

Kite also connects straight to a model — OpenRouter or Nous Portal — using your own API key, with no Hermes agent involved at all. You get a plain chat with no memory, tools or access to your files, which is a different thing from an agent, but it works on its own and it works anywhere. Many of the models are free.

You can switch between the two at any time. Your agent and your direct connection are kept separate, so setting one up never disturbs the other.

What you need

  • Hermes agent running on your own Mac, with its API server enabled.
  • Kite Helper, a small menu-bar app that holds the connection.
  • An iPhone or iPad.
