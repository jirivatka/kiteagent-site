Support

Kite talks either to a model endpoint of your choosing, or to an agent running on your own machine. Almost every problem is one of three: reaching the thing you are talking to, picking a model it can actually serve, or notifications. Start here.

Ask in the support forum — questions and answers stay public, so the next person with the same problem finds it.

If you write in about a connection problem, open Settings → Connection log in Kite, tap Copy all, and paste it into your message. It records which address the app tried and what came back, and it usually identifies the problem outright.

Getting started

Talking to a model

The fastest way in, and nothing to install on any computer.

  1. Settings → Connect to → A model directly.
  2. Tap Use OpenRouter (or type any OpenAI-compatible base URL) and paste your API key.
  3. Pick a model. Free ones are badged.

To use a model on your own computer instead, put its address in the same field and leave the key empty — for Ollama that is http://<address>:11434/v1. See my local model is not answering if it does not respond.

Talking to your agent

For tools, memory and access to your files, you need an agent running somewhere.

  1. Install Kite Helper on the machine that runs your agent, and leave it running.
  2. Open Kite and tap Pair with your Mac.
  3. Scan the code the helper shows.

That is the whole setup. No account, no server address to type, no port forwarding.

The dot is red

On the same wi-fi

  • Check Kite Helper is still running on your Mac, and that the Mac is awake.
  • Confirm both devices are on the same network. A phone on a guest network, or on a different band that the router isolates, cannot reach your Mac.
  • In Kite, go to Settings → Find it again. If your Mac’s address changed, this finds it.

Away from home

Kite reaches your Mac from elsewhere over a private network such as Tailscale. Both devices need it installed and signed in to the same account. Check Settings → Your Mac: if it says “Only works on your wi-fi”, your pairing has no away-from-home address — scan the code in Kite Helper again to add one.

⚠️ If you use a firewall such as Little Snitch, it may silently block incoming connections to Kite Helper, with no prompt and no visible error. Everything works at home and nothing works away from it.

The fix is one rule: allow incoming TCP to Kite Helper on its listening port, from your local network and from 100.64.0.0/10 — the range private networks like Tailscale use. A rule limited to one address will break when that address changes.

Notifications

Kite can tell you when your agent finishes a reply while your phone is away.

  • If nothing arrives, check iOS Settings → Notifications → Kite and make sure Notification Center is switched on — it is separate from Lock Screen and Banners, and with it off notifications vanish after the banner instead of being kept.
  • Set Banner Style → Persistent if you want banners to stay until you dismiss them. Apps cannot set this for you.
  • If you wear an Apple Watch and your phone is locked, notifications go to the watch instead. That is iOS behaviour, not a Kite setting.

My local model is not answering

Nearly always one of two things.

  • It is only listening to itself. Ollama binds 127.0.0.1 by default, which your phone cannot reach. Start it on an address your phone can: OLLAMA_HOST=<your-private-address>:11434 ollama serve.
  • The address is wrong. It must end in /v1 — http://100.x.y.z:11434/v1 — and your phone must be on the same private network as that machine.

Leave the API key empty; local servers do not want one. ⚠️ Do not bind it to 0.0.0.0 to make it work — Ollama has no authentication, so that offers your models to every device on whatever network you are on.

“Today’s free requests are used up”

A free provider key has a small daily allowance, and every step your agent takes is a request — one question that reads a few files can be twenty. The allowance resets at midnight UTC. Most providers raise it substantially for a small one-off credit purchase, and you carry on using free models.

Why did another model answer?

When you connect directly to a provider, Kite asks for the model you chose and lists one or two alternates. If your model is busy — free models are often heavily used — the provider answers with the next one instead, and Kite tells you which. You get a reply rather than an error, and it is always labelled.

Why don’t I see what my agent is doing?

Kite shows your agent’s steps as it works — searching, reading, running a command. If you edit a message in a thread, that thread detaches from your agent’s own copy of the conversation, and its steps stop appearing there. The thread still works. New conversations are unaffected, and Kite says so at the bottom of an edited thread.

Requirements

  • An iPhone or iPad.
  • Somewhere to send messages: a provider API key, a model on your own machine, or an agent.
  • For an agent: Kite Helper on the machine it runs on.
  • Tailscale on both devices, unless you are only using a hosted provider — that works anywhere with nothing extra.

Still stuck

Start a discussion with the connection log and a note of what you were doing. Include whether you were at home or away, and whether the agent works from your Mac itself.
